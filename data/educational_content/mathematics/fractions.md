# Arithmetic - Fractions

## Section: Simplifying Fractions

To simplify a fraction, divide both the numerator and denominator by their
greatest common divisor (GCD). For example, to simplify 8/12, the GCD of 8 and
12 is 4. Dividing both by 4 gives 2/3, which is the simplified form.

## Section: Common Mistakes

Students often simplify only the numerator or only the denominator, which
changes the value of the fraction. Both parts must be divided by the same number.
