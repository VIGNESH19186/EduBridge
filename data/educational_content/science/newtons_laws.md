# Physics - Newton's Laws of Motion

## Section: The Three Laws

Newton's First Law states that an object at rest stays at rest, and an object in
motion stays in motion at constant velocity, unless acted upon by a net external
force. This is also called the law of inertia.

Newton's Second Law states that force equals mass times acceleration (F = ma).
The acceleration of an object depends on the net force acting on it and its mass.

Newton's Third Law states that for every action, there is an equal and opposite
reaction.

## Section: Common Mistakes

A common mistake is confusing mass and weight. Mass is a measure of matter in an
object, while weight is the force of gravity acting on that mass. Another common
mistake is forgetting that when no net force acts on an object (first law), its
velocity — not just its speed — remains unchanged, including direction.
