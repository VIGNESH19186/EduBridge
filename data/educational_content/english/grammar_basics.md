# English - Grammar Basics

## Section: Sentence Structure

A complete sentence requires a subject and a predicate (verb). A common
mistake for learners is writing sentence fragments, which are missing either
the subject or a complete verb phrase.

## Section: Verb Tenses

English uses different verb tenses to indicate when an action occurs: past,
present, and future, each with simple, continuous, and perfect forms. A common
mistake is mixing tenses within a single sentence without a clear reason.
